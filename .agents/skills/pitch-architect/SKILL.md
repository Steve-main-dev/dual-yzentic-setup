# Pitch Architect (Hackathon Edition)

Hackathons are rarely won by lines of code—they are won by the clarity of the problem, the punchiness of the live demo, and the story told to the judges. This skill turns your built MVP into a winning presentation.

## The Winning 3-Minute Hackathon Formula

1. **The Hook & Problem (0:00 - 0:45):**
   - Open with a visceral, relatable problem or stat.
   - Introduce the villain (the status quo, inefficiency, cost, pain point).
2. **The Reveal & Solution (0:45 - 1:15):**
   - Introduce your project in one memorable sentence ("The X for Y that does Z").
   - State the core technological or product breakthrough.
3. **The Live Demo (1:15 - 2:15):**
   - Never show registration/login forms unless auth *is* the product.
   - Start right in the hero screen.
   - Show the single most impressive "WOW" feature first.
   - Complete one end-to-end user loop.
4. **Architecture & Tech Stack (2:15 - 2:35):**
   - Quick visual slide (use `archify` diagram) showing system components, AI pipelines, or integrations.
5. **Business Potential & Vision (2:35 - 3:00):**
   - Who buys this? Why now? What's the 6-month roadmap?
   - Strong closing line.

## Core Deliverables Generated:
- **Slide Deck Outline (Markdown or Marp/Slidev format)**
- **Click-by-Click Live Demo Runbook** (with contingency plans if live API drops)
- **Top 5 Tough Judge Questions & Winning Answers**
